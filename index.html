Hackathon project · 2 weeks

A city that reacts to what happens in it.

I built this prototype in two weeks. The NPCs have their own histories, and I connected their conversations to Gemini so they can respond to the player, the weather and events around town.

I also wrote the traffic system—routes, stops and parking—and a cloud and rain shader controlled by the game. It’s rough in places, as a two-week build should be, but the systems are there and running together.

A little about me

I’m Roman Matskailo.

I work across PC, mobile and web games. My projects range from a visual novel to arcade games, a reactive city and a procedurally generated 3D world. I enjoy that range: each project brings a different problem to solve.

When I’m the only programmer, I take care of the less visible part of the job too—tracking what’s done, explaining decisions and making it easier for someone else to pick up the work.
